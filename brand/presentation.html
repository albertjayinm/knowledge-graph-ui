What we heard

The brief

A 3D, kinetic front end for any knowledge graph, used by developers and by AI coding agents. It should feel kinetic, luminous, connected and precise, without brains, sparkles or the generic three-dot share icon.

kineticluminousconnectedprecise

Success: says 3D graph at a glance, distinct from network clichés, readable at 16 px with the small cut, works in one colour and reversed.

Concept A

Depth

A four-node pyramid graph whose back link breaks where it passes behind the front one, so it reads in 3D; the front node is the one in focus.

  • Only mark in the category that shows a graph in space rather than on a flat page
  • The coloured front node mirrors the app's focus/selection state
  • Small-size cut drops the back link and thickens strokes for 16 px
